Optimisation • Production • Logistics

Alex Abreu is an Assistant Professor of Operational Research at the Faculty of Sciences and Technology (FCT) of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG). He holds a Master's degree in Production Engineering from the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) and is currently pursuing a PhD from the same institution. He completed a sandwich master's degree at the School of Management at the University of Bath (UK). He holds a bachelor's degree in Production Engineering from UFG. Alex has experience in Operational Research with focus on vehicle routing, production planning and scheduling, optimization under uncertainty and exact methods.
